

# Macroeconomic Features

## Overview

This dataset includes **macroeconomic and financial indicators** to capture global economic conditions that influence **weekly realized volatility (RV) of wheat prices**.

It combines:

* **Equity market indicators** (overall market performance and sentiment)
* **Energy market indicators** (input cost and inflation signals)
* **Currency strength measures** (global trade and pricing effects)
* **Market uncertainty indices** (forward-looking risk perception)

The goal is to model how **macroeconomic conditions and global financial signals propagate into commodity volatility**, complementing market-based and climate-based features.

---

## Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJIA Index)

The **Dow Jones Industrial Average** is a major U.S. stock market index representing 30 large publicly traded companies.

### Description

* Price-weighted index of large-cap U.S. companies
* Reflects overall **equity market performance**
* Serves as a proxy for **economic health and investor sentiment**

### Interpretation

* Rising → strong economic outlook, positive sentiment
* Falling → economic slowdown or risk-off behavior

### Why it matters

* Influences:

  * Investment flows into commodities
  * Risk appetite
* Helps explain volatility driven by **global market sentiment**

---

## Crude Oil Prices (WTI Index)

The **West Texas Intermediate** is a key global oil price benchmark.

### Description

* Represents the price of U.S. crude oil
* A major input cost for:

  * Transportation
  * Fertilizers
  * Agricultural production

### Interpretation

* Rising oil prices → higher production and logistics costs
* Falling oil prices → reduced cost pressures

### Why it matters

* Impacts:

  * Agricultural supply costs
  * Inflation expectations
* Can drive commodity volatility via **cost and supply channels**

---

## Broad Dollar Index

The **U.S. Dollar Index** measures the strength of the U.S. dollar relative to a basket of currencies.

### Description

* Trade-weighted representation of USD value
* Reflects global demand for U.S. currency

### Interpretation

* Strong dollar → commodities become more expensive globally
* Weak dollar → commodities become cheaper internationally

### Why it matters

* Agricultural commodities are priced in USD
* Affects:

  * Export competitiveness
  * Global demand
  * Price volatility

---

## Stock Market Uncertainty

The **Stock Market Uncertainty Index** captures uncertainty and risk perception in financial markets.

### Description

* Derived from market volatility and uncertainty signals
* Reflects **investor expectations about future risk**

### Interpretation

* Higher values → increased uncertainty and risk aversion
* Lower values → stable and predictable market conditions

### Why it matters

* Drives:

  * Volatility clustering
  * Sudden market reactions
* Helps explain volatility spikes not captured by price or climate data
